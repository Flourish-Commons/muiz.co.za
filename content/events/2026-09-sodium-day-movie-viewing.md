Sodium Day is Riaz Solker’s first feature film. As he describes it, “it is my love letter to the unique, amazing and heartbreaking time I spent teaching at high schools in South Africa.” Riaz has written, directed, produced, acted and worked in stunts across a variety of local and international film and TV productions. He lectured at AFDA and was head of Film at Varsity College 2006-2014. He currently mentors film students at UCT, works in productions and occasionally teaches Maths at school and university. Soup will be on sale at the screening.
